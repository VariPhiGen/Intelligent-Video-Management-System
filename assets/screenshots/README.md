# Product screenshots

Real screens captured from a running Variphi VMS deployment, using Chrome with a
1600-pixel viewport, then sanitized for publication: every person in the footage
is pixelated and the footage softened, burned-in camera text and wall signage
are blurred, and site-specific camera names and the camera IP are replaced.
Analytics counts, charts and event markers are as captured. These images
document one deployment; its camera inventory and readings are not product
limits or benchmarks.

To refresh, sign in to a deployment with video and analytics history, open the
views above, wait for footage and charts to load, and capture the browser
viewport without browser chrome. Sanitize every capture that shows footage or
camera names before committing it: people, burned-in text, signage, camera
names and addresses. Keep the camera names, capture dates, and
captions in the root README aligned with the replacement images. Use a browser
that supports the camera's video codec.
